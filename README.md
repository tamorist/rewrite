# Rewrite the Rules / ルールを書き換える

Live site: <https://tamorist.github.io/rewrite/>

A single-page introduction to the proposal *Steering Within Limits* (<https://tamorist.github.io/steering/>): why decades of effort have not bent the emissions curve (the rules make reducing a losing move, and carbon prices keep getting repealed), and a package of three proposals to rewrite those rules so that reducing pays — **Planetary OS** (a one-way upstream cap on fossil fuels, 90% of auction revenue returned to citizens, a levy on past beneficiaries, a double key against backsliding, and a climate club worth joining), the **Youth GCC** (a youth-led global citizens' congress with an assembly chosen by lot), and the **Global Citizen Will Platform** (federating movements and connecting their support to decisions). All proposal figures are illustrative; our own illustration is labelled as such, and sources are listed on the page.
